1. Who is responsible

This application, Hermes Personal Agent, is operated by and for a single individual and is used only with that individual's own Google account. The operator and data controller is:

Anthony Puech, Brussels, Belgium
Contact: anthonypuech30@gmail.com

The application is private and self-hosted. It has no other users, no sign-up, and offers no service to the public.

2. What Google user data is accessed

Google serviceData accessedWhy
Gmail Message metadata (sender, recipients, subject, date, labels) and message content, including attachments when the owner asks about one. To let the owner search, summarise and read his own mailbox through the assistant, and to send messages the owner has explicitly approved.
Google Calendar Calendar events: titles, times, locations, descriptions, attendees. To let the owner view upcoming appointments and create, move or cancel events he has explicitly approved.

No other Google service is accessed. The application does not request Drive, Contacts, Sheets, Docs, or any Google permission beyond the two listed above.

3. How the data is used

Data is used for one purpose only: answering the owner's own requests about his own email and calendar, and carrying out actions on them that the owner has approved. Examples: finding a message, listing the week's appointments, creating a reminder.

4. What is never done with the data

  • No selling, renting or transferring of Google user data to third parties.
  • No use for advertising, marketing, profiling, credit or lending decisions.
  • No use to train or improve machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models.
  • No human other than the owner reads the data.
  • No automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Where the data is stored

The application runs on infrastructure controlled by the owner. The OAuth token that grants access is stored as a local file on that infrastructure, readable only by the owner's account, and is encrypted in transit. Message and calendar content is processed in memory and is not copied into any cloud database, and no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind is included.

When the owner asks the assistant a question, the relevant content may be sent to the language-model provider configured by the owner for the sole purpose of producing that answer. No Google user data is used by that provider to train models, and the owner can change or remove that provider at any time.

6. Retention

Google user data is retained only as long as needed to serve the owner's request. Access can be revoked at any moment (see section 8), which immediately ends the application's ability to reach the account.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use

Hermes Personal Agent's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Your rights

The only person whose data is processed is the owner, who already controls it directly. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately and permanently stops the application's access. Any question about this data can be sent to the contact address above.

9. Children

The application is not directed at children and processes no data relating to children.
